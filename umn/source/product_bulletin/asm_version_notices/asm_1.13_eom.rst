ASM 1.13 EOM
============

**Released**: June 8, 2026

ASM 1.13 has reached end of maintenance (EOM) on January 31, 2026, 00:00 GMT+08:00. As of this date, new service meshes of ASM 1.13 and earlier versions will no longer be available. If you have service meshes of version 1.13 or earlier, you are advised to upgrade them to the latest commercial version as soon as possible to ensure your service continuity.
