ASM 1.8 EOM
===========

**Released**: April 24, 2024

ASM 1.8 will reach EOM on April 30, 2024, 00:00 GMT+08:00. By then, you cannot create service meshes 1.8 and earlier versions. Upgrade your service mesh 1.8 and earlier versions to the latest commercial version.
